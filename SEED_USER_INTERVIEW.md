# Seed Test User Interview Guide: Growth

This guide is for one-on-one interviews with Seed Test participants to understand the qualitative value of the **Growth** app.

---

## 1. Initial Impression & Motivation
- **First usage**: What was the primary reason you decided to try this app?
- **Previous methods**: How did you manage your self-discipline or study habits before this?
- **The Struggle**: Why do you usually find it hard to stick to your goals?

## 2. Core Value Validation
- **Daily Tasks**: Did the automatically generated tasks (e.g., Vocabulary, Coding practice) help you get started? Was the time allocation appropriate?
- **Focus Timer**: Did the countdown timer actually help you stay concentrated? Did you notice the timer working correctly in the background?
- **Growth & Gamification**:
    - Does seeing your **XP** and **Level** increase make you want to use the app more?
    - How did it feel when you unlocked an **Achievement**?
    - How important is your **Streak** number to you?

## 3. Companion (Mock) Feedback
- **Usage**: Why did you choose to use (or not use) the "Study Companion" (搭子) feature?
- **Engagement**: Did seeing a partner (even if simulated) "focusing" alongside you change your behavior?
- **Realism**: Did you feel the partner's status updates were motivating or distracting?

## 4. Feature Prioritization & UX
- **Most Valuable**: If you had to pick one feature that provided the most value, what would it be?
- **Least Valuable**: Which feature felt the most useless or confusing?
- **The "Keep One" Test**: If I told you I was going to delete every feature except one, which one would you ask me to keep?

## 5. Closing & Future
- **Retention**: Are you willing to continue using the app tomorrow? Why or why not?
- **Missing Pieces**: What is the one thing you kept looking for but couldn't find in the app?
- **Overall Rating**: On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to recommend this to a friend trying to learn something new?
